Hotel Revenue & Market Share Analysis (Power BI)
**Business Problem:** AtliQ Grands, a luxury hotel chain in India, was losing market share and revenue to competitors. Management lacked a central system to track daily performance, making it difficult to spot pricing inefficiencies or adjust room rates effectively across different cities and booking channels.

**Solution:** Designed an interactive Power BI analytics dashboard to analyze 130k+ booking records and ₹1.69B in revenue across 5 major cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Ahmedabad). The dashboard provides real-time visibility into key hospitality metrics to drive data-backed decision-making.

**Key Insights & Findings:**
Core Metrics Tracked: Evaluated performance using key industry KPIs: RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room), ADR (Average Daily Rate), Occupancy %, and Realisation %.

**Occupancy Gap:** Discovered that weekday occupancy was 55.8% compared to 62.6% on weekends—highlighting a major opportunity to boost weekday business bookings.

**Property & Channel Split:** Identified that Luxury properties generated 61.6% of total revenue versus 38.4% for Business properties, while identifying high-cancellation third-party booking platforms.

**Business Impact:**
Enabled the management team to transition from fixed pricing to a dynamic pricing strategy.

Identified target areas to optimize room rates and occupancy, projecting a 15% gain in overall revenue efficiency.
